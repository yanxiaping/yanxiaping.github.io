Menu Favorites

Supreme Tonkotsu “極上豚骨” (pictured)

Signature creamy pork bone broth served with pork belly, pork loin, and pork chashu. A Meat lovers dream

Ichiraku “一楽 ‘one joy!’”

Narutos favorite ramen! Our creamy pork bone broth with miso paste. Pork Loin, Marinated Egg, Scallions, and bamboo

Shio “塩”

Our simple, salty, and satisfying chicken base ramen. Served with ajitsuke tamago, bean sprouts, chicken gyoza, narutomaki, scallions, sesame seeds, and nori

VEGAN PARADISE “素食天国”

Miso paste, soy sauce, and soy milk mixed into our seaweed soup base. Served with tofu, vegetable gyoza, wood ear mushroom, corn, bean sprouts, scallions, sesame seeds, and nori.
